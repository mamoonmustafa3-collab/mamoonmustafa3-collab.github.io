01 / About me

A marketer with a strategic mindset.

I am a Digital Marketing graduate from The Hashemite University and a Master's student in Digital Marketing at Applied Science Private University, Jordan.

My professional experience brings together social media management, paid advertising, content planning, and commercial video production. I enjoy turning business objectives into clear digital strategies, then translating those strategies into content and campaigns designed for specific audiences.

I believe effective marketing goes beyond publishing content or generating engagement. It requires clear objectives, meaningful KPIs, an understanding of consumer behavior, and a willingness to learn from campaign performance.

Alongside my practical work, I am passionate about marketing research and the academic foundations of consumer decision-making. I aim to connect evidence-based insights with the realities of day-to-day marketing practice.

“Creative ideas attract attention. Strategic execution creates value. Data helps us understand the difference.”
